## Part 1: Software Descriptions (copy-paste or adapt)

### Short (1–2 sentences) — for bios, hooks, or tight character limits
- **"AI-powered brain MRI classifier: tumor vs non-tumor in seconds. Built with PyTorch and ResNet for researchers and developers."**
- **"Classify brain MRI images as tumor or non_tumor with a trained deep learning model. Clean code, ready for research and prototyping."**
- **"Binary brain MRI classification—tumor detection powered by ResNet and PyTorch. For education and research, not clinical diagnosis."**

### Medium (2–4 sentences) — for captions and post body
- **"Our brain MRI classifier uses deep learning (PyTorch + ResNet18) to distinguish tumor from non_tumor in MRI images. It’s built for researchers, students, and developers who need a reliable binary classifier—with clean code, confidence scores, and optional GPU acceleration. Ideal for prototyping and education. Not a medical device; for research use only."**
- **"Tumor vs non_tumor from brain scans—in one pipeline. We train a ResNet model on your dataset (or a public one), deliver the trained model and inference script, and handle imbalanced data with class weights. You get accuracy reports, confusion matrices, and code you can run and modify. Perfect for ML projects and medical imaging research."**

### Long (paragraph) — for Facebook “About” or long-form posts
- **"This software is an end-to-end brain MRI classification pipeline that labels images as tumor or non_tumor. It uses a pretrained ResNet18 model fine-tuned on brain MRI data (e.g. glioma, meningioma, pituitary, no_tumor), with data augmentation, class weighting for imbalanced datasets, and optional mixed-precision GPU training. You receive the full training pipeline, trained model weights, and a prediction script that outputs a label and confidence score per image, with an adjustable threshold to reduce false positives. Built in Python with PyTorch and scikit-learn, it’s designed for researchers, students, and developers—for education and research only, not for clinical diagnosis."**

## Part 2: Prompts to Generate Facebook Posts

Use these as instructions for a copywriter or AI (e.g. “Generate a Facebook post that does the following:”).

---

**Prompt 1 — Problem/solution (educational)**  
*"Write a Facebook post (2–3 short paragraphs) that introduces the problem of quickly screening brain MRI images for tumor vs non_tumor, then presents our software as a solution. Tone: professional but approachable. Include a clear call-to-action (e.g. ‘Message us’ or ‘Link in comments’). End with a one-line disclaimer: for research and education only, not for clinical use."*

---

**Prompt 2 — Tech audience (developers/researchers)**  
*"Write a Facebook post targeting developers and researchers. Highlight: PyTorch, ResNet18, binary classification, confidence threshold, class weights for imbalanced data, optional GPU/AMP. Keep it under 150 words. Use bullet points or short lines. Include a CTA like ‘DM for details’ or ‘Link in bio.’"*

---

**Prompt 3 — Benefit-focused (time/simplicity)**  
*"Write a Facebook post that emphasizes benefits: fast classification, one pipeline from data to trained model, ready-to-run inference script, no need to build from scratch. Tone: helpful and confident. Include one software description sentence (tumor vs non_tumor brain MRI classifier) and a CTA. No medical claims."*

---

**Prompt 4 — Trust / transparency**  
*"Write a Facebook post that builds trust: we deliver clean code, full pipeline, and evaluation metrics (accuracy, confusion matrix). Mention that the model is for research and education only, not a medical device. Tone: honest and professional. Include a short software description and a CTA."*

---

**Prompt 5 — “How it works” (3 steps)**  
*"Write a Facebook post that explains in 3 simple steps how our brain MRI classifier works: (1) You provide or we use a brain MRI dataset in folders by class; (2) We train a ResNet model and give you the pipeline and model; (3) You run the inference script on new images. Keep each step to 1–2 sentences. Add a one-line description of the software and a CTA."*

---

**Prompt 6 — Student/researcher angle**  
*"Write a Facebook post aimed at students and researchers in ML or medical imaging. Offer our brain MRI tumor vs non_tumor classifier as a project solution or research tool. Mention: PyTorch, ResNet, clean code, evaluation report. Tone: supportive and academic. Include a CTA and a brief disclaimer."*

## Part 3: Prompts to Generate Instagram Posts

Use these as instructions for a copywriter or AI.

---

**Prompt 7 — Instagram caption (single image or carousel)**  
*"Write an Instagram caption for a post promoting our brain MRI tumor vs non_tumor classification software. Structure: hook (first line grabs attention), 2–3 short lines describing what it does (binary classification, PyTorch, ResNet), one line on who it’s for (researchers, developers, students), and a CTA (e.g. ‘Link in bio’ or ‘DM for details’). Add 2–3 relevant hashtags at the end. Total length: suitable for Instagram (under 300 words). Tone: clear and professional."*

---

**Prompt 8 — Carousel (slide-by-slide copy)**  
*"Write copy for a 5-slide Instagram carousel promoting our brain MRI classifier. Slide 1: headline (e.g. ‘Tumor vs Non-Tumor in Brain MRI’). Slide 2: what it is (one short paragraph). Slide 3: key features (3–4 bullet points: ResNet, confidence scores, clean code, optional GPU). Slide 4: who it’s for (researchers, students, developers). Slide 5: CTA + disclaimer (research only). Each slide: 1–3 short sentences or bullets. Keep text minimal for readability on mobile."*

---

**Prompt 9 — Reel / short-video script (15–30 sec)**  
*"Write a short script (15–30 seconds when read aloud) for an Instagram Reel promoting our brain MRI tumor vs non_tumor classifier. Structure: hook (0–3 sec), problem (3–8 sec), solution (8–20 sec), CTA (20–30 sec). Mention: binary classification, PyTorch, ResNet, fast inference. Tone: punchy and clear. Include optional on-screen text suggestions (e.g. ‘Tumor vs Non-Tumor’ ‘Built with PyTorch’)."*

---

**Prompt 10 — Instagram Story (3 frames)**  
*"Write copy for 3 consecutive Instagram Story frames. Frame 1: one line hook (e.g. ‘Brain MRI classification in seconds’). Frame 2: one sentence on what the software does + one key feature. Frame 3: CTA (e.g. ‘Swipe up’ or ‘DM us’) + ‘For research only.’ Keep each frame to 1–2 short lines. Casual but professional tone."*

---

**Prompt 11 — Bio / profile description**  
*"Write a 1–2 sentence Instagram bio for a brand that offers a brain MRI tumor vs non_tumor classification software (PyTorch, ResNet, for researchers and developers). Include a clear value proposition and optionally one hashtag. Character limit: under 150."*
